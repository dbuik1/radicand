# Privacy policy

Radicand collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no
analytics and no advertising, and it makes no network requests: everything
it needs ships inside the extension.

## What it stores

Everything below stays in your browser, in the extension's own storage:

- **Your library** of saved formulae and your **custom shortcuts**.
- The **symbols you used most recently**, for the Recent tab.
- Your **settings**, such as theme, text size and which parts of the
  interface are shown.

The library, shortcuts and recent symbols are kept only on this device.
Settings are saved in Chrome's synced extension storage, so if you have
turned on Chrome sync they follow you to your other devices through your
Google account, under Google's privacy policy. The extension never sends
them anywhere itself.

Removing the extension deletes all of it. Export files you create from the
library or shortcuts are saved wherever you choose and are yours.

## What it can access

The extension does not read or change the web pages you visit, and cannot
see your browsing history. It asks for three permissions:

- `sidePanel` – to show the editor in Chrome's side panel.
- `storage` – to keep the data listed above.
- `clipboardWrite` – to put the equation on the clipboard when you choose
  Copy.

## Changes

Any change to this policy is published in this file, with its history in
