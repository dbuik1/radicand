# Security

The extension works offline: it makes no network requests, and what you
type stays in your browser's own storage. The areas where a security
problem could still arise are the files it imports (library and shortcut
JSON), what it copies to the clipboard, and its three permissions
(`sidePanel`, `storage`, `clipboardWrite`).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report it privately through GitHub: on this repository's
**Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**. If you cannot use
GitHub, email contact@davidbuik.com instead. Do not open a public issue. Include the steps to reproduce it and the version of the extension
and browser you used.

You will get a reply acknowledging the report, and a fix will be released
as soon as one is ready. Reports are credited in the release notes unless
you would rather not be named.
